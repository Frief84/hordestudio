# Horde Studio 18.3.7

Worlds recovery, refreshed bundled campaigns, optional mapping tools and more reliable Virtual Human photos.

- Prevent an unreachable named destination from being replaced by a different nearby room.
- Fix Worlds reply repair using the wrong response protocol, redundant same-location movement failures, and compatibility with older maps that have no authored place-awareness rules.
- Recover ordinary prose-only look, greeting and informational replies through a validated unchanged-scene response. Mechanical changes still require valid state updates. Turn timeout errors stay visible with their diagnostic reason.
- Keep **Structured events (existing)** as the default. **Scene record + ledger** remains experimental, with clearly marked provisional conversation and manual scene correction. **Linked actions (preview)** is opt-in, with ordered transactions, movement authorization, co-located item transfers and authored journey durations.
- Add World Lore place guides, floor-plan editing, route waypoints, shared-door controls, bounded source-linked recall and region/location background music. Local uploaded music must remain available on the destination installation.
- Update all four bundled worlds: Vaelora, Bellwether 2005, Aldenmere, and Policy Panic at Bramble & Pike. Repair missing routes, opening locations and cast, daily schedules, and duplicated player-role NPCs. Existing stock records update selectively; customized records are skipped and saved campaign progress is preserved.
- Improve Virtual Human photo requests: preserve the user's requested scene, distinguish ordinary photos from selfies, avoid conflicting clothing/place references, and separate requested and spontaneous daily allowances. Add Natural, Request Only and Photo Friendly settings, protect settings from stale reads, and roll back failed photo queue attempts.
- Restore the AI Horde settings controls and prevent excluded voice controls from breaking Settings.
- Include the new runtime modules in portable packaging and refresh the built-in guide and Pip control reference.

Selected Worlds contributions were adapted from Frief84/hordestudio, with additional integration and safety fixes. Kokoro-specific changes are not included.

Download and extract the complete portable ZIP, then restart Horde Studio and refresh the browser. Back up your library before updating. Existing discarded replies are retained in history; they are not automatically rewritten. Existing campaigns receive eligible template repairs, while new campaigns use the revised openings.

Validation uses offline engine/backend checks, isolated browsers with scripted model responses, and portable-package verification. Live paid model adherence, generated photo quality and uninterrupted long campaigns are not certified by these tests. Linked actions and scene-record mode remain preview/experimental features.
